Leonardo now offers more than twenty image models: its own (Lucid, Phoenix and older presets such as Kino XL and Anime XL) plus partner models such as FLUX, GPT Image, Nano Banana, Ideogram and Seedream. This page tells you which one to pick for each job, what free users can use, and gives a prompt to start with for each.

## Quick pick

| You want... | Use | Why (from Leonardo's docs) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A good default for almost anything | **Lucid Origin** | Leonardo's most adaptable, prompt-responsive model; Full HD output; wide range of styles |
| Cinematic photos, frames for video | **Lucid Realism** | Built for cinematic image-to-video input: natural lighting, subtle expressions, mood |
| Long, detailed prompts followed closely; text in images | **Phoenix 1.0** | Strong prompt adherence, coherent text even in sentences; on the Free plan |
| Anime and stylized art | **Lucid Origin** with "anime" in the prompt, or **Anime XL** | Leonardo's guide recommends Lucid Origin for anime; Anime XL is the stylized Lightning model |
| Studio Ghibli-like images | **GPT Image** | Leonardo's model guide recommends a GPT Image model for Ghibli-style images |
| Posters, logos, T-shirts, book covers | **Ideogram** | Leonardo's guide calls it the typography specialist |
| Editing a photo with words, combining reference images | **Nano Banana**, **GPT Image**, **FLUX.1 Kontext**, **Seedream** | "Omni" models: up to 6 reference images and the Inline Editor |
| Fast sketches and drafts | **FLUX Schnell** or **Leonardo Lightning** | Fast; Leonardo says Schnell is not meant for final renders |
| Training your own character or style | **FLUX Dev** | Leonardo's guide names it for custom characters and styles |

## Leonardo's own models

### Lucid Origin

Leonardo's help center describes Lucid Origin as its most adaptable and prompt-responsive model: vibrant color, diverse characters, accurate text and native Full HD. Its tips: short prompts work, but add cues for the look you want. For photos, describe lighting, lens and mood; for design, give clear typography directions; for illustration, name the medium.

```text
cinematic photo of a street food vendor at night, steam rising from the grill, neon reflections on wet pavement, 35mm lens, shallow depth of field
```

```text
poster design, bold sans serif headline "NIGHT MARKET" centered at the top, minimal palette of orange and deep blue, flat illustration of lanterns below
```

Leonardo's model guide notes it can look overly polished; add words such as "film grain" or "analog photo" if you want a rougher look.

### Lucid Realism

Built for cinematic image-to-video input. It is strongest at natural lighting, subtle expressions, mood and atmosphere, so use it when the image will become the first frame of a video, or when you want a quiet, filmic photo.

```text
close-up of an elderly fisherman looking out at the sea, soft overcast light, subtle smile, wind in his hair, cinematic, muted colors
```

### Phoenix 1.0 and 0.9

Leonardo's foundational model. It follows long, detailed instructions closely and renders coherent text, even full sentences. Leonardo's model guide suggests Quality mode rather than Fast for best results, and finds it good for stylized assets and sticker-style vector art. Phoenix is available on every web plan, including Free, and it is one of the models that accepts a negative prompt.

```text
die-cut sticker of a cheerful red panda holding a coffee cup, thick white border, flat vector style, the words "MONDAY MODE" in rounded letters underneath
```

### Leonardo Lightning and Anime XL

The Lightning XL family is built for speed: up to 2–3× faster generations at a lower token cost. There are two models: **Leonardo Lightning XL** for photorealistic images and **Leonardo Anime XL** for stylized images. You cannot change the scheduler or guidance scale on them. For best results Leonardo recommends at least 1024 px on one side and Alchemy V2 on.

```text
anime illustration of a girl with short silver hair on a rooftop at sunset, school uniform, wind blowing cherry blossoms, vibrant colors, detailed background
```

### Other Leonardo presets

Cinematic Kino, Concept Art, Graphic Design, Illustrative Albedo, Lifelike Vision, Portrait Perfect and Stock Photography are older Leonardo presets. They are still available, accept negative prompts, and run under Unlimited generations on Premium and Ultimate plans.

## Partner models on Leonardo

| Model | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **Nano Banana** (Gemini 2.5 Flash Image), **Nano Banana Pro** (newer: Nano Banana 2) | Precise edits to parts of an image, photo restoration, colorizing drawings and manga panels, up to 6 reference images |
| **GPT Image 1.5 / 2 / 2.5** | Omni model for generation and edits with references; Ghibli-style images per Leonardo's guide |
| **FLUX.1 Kontext, FLUX.2 Pro** | Editing and combining reference images (Omni models) |
| **FLUX Dev / FLUX Schnell** | Custom style and character training / fast drafts |
| **Ideogram 3.0, 4.5** | Typography: posters, logos, T-shirts, covers |
| **Seedream 4.0 / 4.5** (newer: 5.0 Pro, 5.0 Flash) | Omni-model generation and editing with references |
| **Krea 2, Recraft V4** | Newer additions; check the model's page in the app for details |

"Omni" models are Leonardo's name for models with stronger understanding of both image and text inputs. You can use them to generate from scratch or to edit with the **Inline Editor**: click an image, describe the change, and add references.

```text
Nano Banana edit: replace the grey sky with a warm sunset, keep the people and the building exactly the same
```

```text
GPT Image: a cozy countryside bakery in a soft hand-painted animated film style, warm afternoon light, a cat sleeping on the windowsill
```

## Free plan: what you can use

- Free accounts get **150 tokens a day**. Unused tokens do not carry over to the next day.
- Every model has its own cost, shown on the Generate button before you click. Image size, number of images, mode (Fast/Ultra) and reference images change it.
- **Phoenix** is available on the Free plan.
- **Nano Banana** is open to free users at 40 tokens per image (with 1 reference image instead of 6), so 150 tokens cover 3 images a day.
- **Lucid Origin** is available with a Leonardo account; its cost depends on mode and image size.
- Paid plans add premium models and features, monthly tokens with a rollover bank, and top-ups.

### Unlimited generations (paid plans)

Premium plans can keep generating images, and Ultimate plans images and videos, after tokens run out, at slower speed. Supported image models: Lucid Origin, Lucid Realism, Phoenix 1.0, Phoenix 0.9, FLUX Dev, FLUX Schnell, Anime, Cinematic Kino, Concept Art, Graphic Design, Illustrative Albedo, Lifelike Vision, Leonardo Lightning, Portrait Perfect and Stock Photography.

## Model settings and negative prompts

The side bar settings vary by model. Negative prompts in particular are only available on some models: Phoenix, Ideogram 3.0 and the older presets such as Kino XL and Anime XL have one; Lucid, FLUX, GPT Image, Nano Banana and Seedream do not. The full table and copy-paste lists are in the [Leonardo negative prompts guide](/blog/2026-02-22-master-leonardo-ai-negative-prompts).

For keeping the same character across images, see the [Leonardo consistent characters guide](/blog/2026-01-06-master-consistent-characters-leonardo-ai). For Ghibli-style prompt wording, see the [Studio Ghibli prompts guide](/blog/2026-07-01-master-studio-ghibli-ai-art-prompts).
