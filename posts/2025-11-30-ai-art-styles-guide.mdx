A style keyword tells the model what kind of picture to make: an oil painting, a pixel art sprite, a 1970s film photo. This list has 70 styles in five groups. Each row gives keywords you can copy, and each group ends with a full example prompt.

The keywords describe the visual traits of a style instead of only naming it. "Impressionist painting" alone works, but "impressionist painting, visible brushstrokes, dappled light" gets you closer, more consistently.

## How to use a style keyword

```text
[subject], [style keywords], [color or light]
```

```text
a lighthouse on a rocky coast, impressionist painting, visible brushstrokes, dappled light, soft pastel colors
```

Put the subject first and the style after it. If the style is weak, move it to the front or repeat its key trait.

## Mixing styles

Two styles can work together when one sets the **medium** and the other sets the **theme**:

```text
[subject], [medium style], [theme style]
```

```text
a futuristic city skyline, ukiyo-e woodblock print, cyberpunk neon signs
```

```text
a robot gardener, watercolor painting, solarpunk greenery
```

```text
a knight in armor, pixel art, Art Nouveau floral frame
```

Three or more styles usually blend into a vague average. If a style disappears, give it more weight: in Stable Diffusion UIs, `(watercolor painting:1.3)`; in Midjourney, put it at the start of the prompt.

## Tips

- **Describe the traits, not just the name.** "Art Deco" plus "geometric shapes, gold and black" is more reliable than "Art Deco" alone.
- **Match style and subject.** A photography style needs a photographable scene; a pixel art style works better with simple subjects.
- **Use the negative prompt to stop style drift.** For an illustration, add `photograph, photorealistic, 3d render` to the negative field. See the [negative prompt lists](/blog/2025-11-29-stable-diffusion-negative-prompts-guide).
- **Movements over living artists.** Naming movements and techniques gives you the look without copying a specific living artist's work.
- **Expect differences between models.** Anime checkpoints do anime better than base models; photo checkpoints resist painterly styles. For SDXL specifics, see the [SDXL prompt guide](/blog/2026-05-17-master-sdxl-prompting-guide).
