"Ghibli style" usually means a specific feeling: hand-painted backgrounds, huge soft clouds, everyday life drawn with care, and a touch of quiet magic. Typing the studio name alone tends to give a generic anime image. Describing those traits gives a much closer result, and it also works on generators that filter studio or artist names.

## The look in words

| Trait | Prompt words |
| --- | --- |
| Backgrounds | hand-painted watercolor and gouache background, detailed lush scenery |
| Sky | towering cumulus clouds, bright blue summer sky, soft sunlight |
| Color | warm natural palette, saturated greens, soft pastel light |
| Line | clean simple character outlines, cel-shaded characters on painted backgrounds |
| Life | everyday moments, laundry on the line, steaming food, wind in the grass |
| Magic | small forest spirits, flying machines, gentle giant creatures |
| Mood | nostalgic, peaceful, warm, a sense of wonder |

## Base style prompt

```text
hand-drawn 2D anime film still, hand-painted watercolor background, soft natural light, warm nostalgic atmosphere, detailed lush scenery, cel-shaded characters
```

Add `in the style of Studio Ghibli` at the end if your generator accepts it; the descriptive part does most of the work either way.

## Tips

- **Describe, don't just name.** The table traits above matter more than the studio name.
- **Background first.** In this style the scenery is the star; give it most of the prompt.
- **Add weather and time.** Summer clouds, rain at a bus stop, a sunset over the sea: see the [weather and time-of-day prompts](/blog/2026-02-24-master-ai-art-weather-time-prompts).
- **Keep characters simple.** Plain clothes, simple faces and natural poses; rule out glossy modern anime in the negative field.
- **Respect the source.** The films, characters and names belong to Studio Ghibli. Use these prompts for your own original scenes, not to copy characters or to pass images off as studio work.
- **Explore similar looks.** Watercolor, gouache and storybook illustration combine well; see the [AI art styles list](/blog/2025-11-30-ai-art-styles-guide).
