**Status, October 2026:** OpenAI removed the `dall-e-2` and `dall-e-3` models from its API on May 12, 2026, after announcing it on November 14, 2025. OpenAI's deprecations page lists `gpt-image-2`, `gpt-image-1` and `gpt-image-1-mini` as replacements, and some GPT Image models have their own removal dates (for example `gpt-image-1` on October 23, 2026). Check that page before you build on a specific model.

The prompts themselves still work. Full sentences that describe a photograph carry over from DALL·E 3 to GPT Image and to other modern generators. Every prompt below is written that way, with a copy button.

## The photo prompt formula

```text
A [shot type] photo of [subject doing something] in [place]. [Light]. Shot on [lens / camera feel], [depth of field]. [Color and film look]. [Small real-world details].
```

| Part | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Shot type | close-up portrait, candid street photo, overhead product shot, wide landscape photo |
| Light | soft window light, overcast daylight, golden hour backlight, single bare bulb |
| Lens | 35mm, 50mm, 85mm portrait lens, 100mm macro, 24mm wide-angle |
| Depth of field | shallow depth of field, background softly blurred, everything in focus |
| Color / film | natural colors, muted tones, slight film grain, Kodak Portra-like warmth |
| Real details | visible skin texture, flyaway hairs, scuffs on the table, condensation on the glass |

The last row matters most. Real photos are full of small imperfections; images without them look like renders.

## Portraits

```text
A close-up portrait photo of a 70-year-old fisherman with a weathered face and a grey beard, standing on a harbor at dawn. Soft overcast light. Shot on an 85mm lens, background softly blurred. Natural colors, visible skin texture and pores, a few water droplets on his jacket.
```

```text
A candid photo of a young woman laughing at a cafe table by a large window. Soft daylight from the side. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field. Muted warm tones, slight film grain, a half-finished cup of coffee in the foreground.
```

```text
A professional headshot of a man in his forties wearing a navy blazer and white shirt, plain light grey background. Soft studio lighting from the front left. 85mm lens. Natural skin tones, no retouching look.
```

```text
An environmental portrait of a potter at her wheel in a cluttered studio. Light from a single high window. 35mm lens, clay dust in the air, clay on her hands and apron.
```

## Street and documentary

```text
A candid street photo of a busy night market in the rain. Neon signs reflect on wet pavement, people with umbrellas pass by in motion blur. 35mm lens, high contrast, slight grain.
```

```text
A documentary-style photo of a train conductor leaning out of an old train window at a rural station. Late afternoon light. 35mm lens, faded colors, dust on the window frame.
```

A lifestyle product photo of a ceramic coffee mug on a wooden desk next to an open notebook. Morning window light, steam rising from the mug. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field.

An overhead flat-lay photo of a leather wallet, keys, sunglasses and a watch on a linen cloth. Soft even daylight. Everything in focus, neutral colors.

A close-up photo of a bowl of ramen with a soft-boiled egg, sliced pork and green onions. Steam rising, side light from a window. 50mm lens, shallow depth of field, dark wooden table, small drips of broth on the rim.

An overhead photo of a rustic breakfast table: sourdough toast, butter, berries and coffee. Soft morning light, crumbs on the table, natural colors.

## Tips for realism

- **Describe a photo, not quality.** "Shot on a 50mm lens in soft window light" helps more than a pile of words like "ultra realistic, 8k, masterpiece".
- **Add one or two imperfections.** Dust, scuffs, flyaway hairs, condensation, uneven light.
- **Name the light source.** "Light from a single window on the left" is more realistic than "beautiful lighting".
- **Write exclusions as sentences.** "No text or logos anywhere in the image" works in sentence-based models.
- **Keep one lens and one light.** Mixed camera and lighting cues give flat, average images. The [lens prompt cheat sheet](/blog/2026-02-16-master-ai-art-lenses-prompts-guide) and the [weather and time-of-day prompts](/blog/2026-02-24-master-ai-art-weather-time-prompts) have more options.
