Pen and ink has a small vocabulary that changes the result a lot: the tool (pen, brush, marker), the way tone is built (hatching, cross-hatching, stippling, wash) and how much is left white. Pick one of each and the model has a clear target.

## Quick reference

| Prompt term | What you get | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| clean line art | Outlines only, no shading | Coloring pages, logos, tattoos |
| hatching | Parallel lines for shadow | Classic illustration, portraits |
| cross-hatching | Layered crossing lines, deep shadows | Engravings, dramatic scenes |
| stippling | Tone built from dots | Botanical and scientific drawings |
| ink wash | Watered-down ink in soft grey tones | Landscapes, moody scenes |
| brush and ink | Thick-to-thin expressive strokes | Comics, calligraphic sketches |
| fineliner sketch | Thin even lines, loose construction | Urban sketching, sketchbooks |
| scratchboard | White lines cut from black | High-contrast animals and portraits |

Tate's glossary notes that ink was historically applied with brushes, quills, reed pens and later metal pens, and that pen and ink is often combined with washes. These tools are useful prompt words in their own right: `quill pen`, `reed pen`, `dip pen`, `sable brush`.

## Base prompts

### Clean line art

```text
clean black ink line art of a mountain village, uniform line weight, no shading, pure white background, minimal detail
```

### Hatching

```text
pen and ink drawing of an old fisherman mending a net, fine parallel hatching for shadows, black ink on white paper, detailed illustration
```

### Cross-hatching

```text
detailed cross-hatching ink illustration of a gothic cathedral facade, deep shadows built from layered lines, engraving style, black and white
```

### Stippling

```text
stippling ink drawing of a snail on a leaf, tone made entirely of tiny dots, scientific illustration style, white background
```

### Ink wash

```text
ink wash painting of a misty river valley, soft grey tones, a few dark brush accents, lots of empty white paper
```

### Brush and ink

```text

```

### Sketchbook

```text

```

### Scratchboard

```text
scratchboard illustration of an owl, fine white lines carved from a black surface, strong contrast, intricate feather texture
```

## Adding a touch of color

Ink drawings often carry a single accent or a light watercolor wash:

```text
pen and ink drawing of a lighthouse on a cliff with loose watercolor washes, mostly black line work, soft blue and ochre accents
```

```text
black ink sketch of a city skyline with a single red accent on one umbrella, everything else black and white
```

## Negative prompt

If the model keeps adding color or grey shading, use these in the negative field:

```text
color, colored, grayscale shading, photo, 3d render, gradient, blurry lines, smudged
```

For clean line art, also add `hatching, shading, texture`. More lists are in the [negative prompt guide](/blog/2025-11-29-stable-diffusion-negative-prompts-guide).

## Tips

- **Name the paper.** `white paper`, `cream sketchbook paper` or `toned paper` change the overall feel.
- **Say what stays white.** `lots of white space` or `white sky` stops the model from filling every area.
- **Control line weight.** `uniform line weight` for technical drawings, `varied line weight` for lively sketches.
- **Use resolution for detail.** Fine hatching needs pixels. For SDXL, generate at native resolution; see the [SDXL prompt guide](/blog/2026-05-17-master-sdxl-prompting-guide).
- **Combine with another style.** Ink works with ukiyo-e, comics and botanical illustration. See the [AI art styles list](/blog/2025-11-30-ai-art-styles-guide).
