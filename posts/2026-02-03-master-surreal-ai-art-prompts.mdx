Surrealism is about the impossible shown as if it were ordinary. The most striking surreal AI images are not the busiest ones. They take a believable scene and break one rule: scale, gravity, material or logic. Every prompt below has a copy button; swap in your own subject.

## The surreal formula

```text
[ordinary scene], [one impossible element], [realistic rendering], [quiet dreamlike light]
```

| Rule to break | Example element |
| --- | --- |
| Scale | a giant teacup used as a swimming pool |
| Gravity | rain falling upward from a puddle |
| Material | a staircase made of folded paper |
| Inside / outside | a room with the ocean as its ceiling |
| Body | a person whose head is a birdcage |
| Time | one half of the street in winter, the other in summer |

Keep the rendering realistic and calm. The contrast between a believable photo and an impossible idea is what makes the image feel like a dream rather than a cartoon.

## Movement-inspired prompts

The Tate's glossary lists Salvador Dalí, René Magritte, Max Ernst, Joan Miró, Yves Tanguy, Leonora Carrington and Dorothea Tanning among the artists of the movement. Describe the visual traits rather than leaning on a name alone:

| Inspired by | Visual traits to describe |
| --- | --- |
| Magritte | everyday objects in the wrong place, clean flat light, bowler hats, clouds, calm |
| Dalí | melting forms, vast empty plains, long shadows, hyper-detailed rendering |
| Tanguy | smooth abstract shapes on an endless horizon, muted grey-blue tones |
| Ernst | forest textures, rubbed and scraped surfaces, strange hybrid creatures |
| Carrington | mythical animals, figures in long robes, dreamlike rituals |
| Miró | floating symbols, thin lines, bright primary colors, playful shapes |

```text
an everyday umbrella standing on its own in an empty room with a cloudy sky painted on the walls, flat clean light, quiet surrealism in the style of René Magritte
```

```text
an endless plain under a pale sky, smooth abstract stone forms casting long shadows, muted grey-blue palette, inspired by Yves Tanguy
```

## Tips

- **Break one rule, not five.** A single impossible element in a realistic scene reads as surreal. Many at once read as random.
- **Keep the light calm.** Soft, even light makes the impossible look matter-of-fact, which is the point of surrealism.
- **Use empty space.** Deserts, empty rooms, plain skies and long shadows give the strange element room to stand out.
- **Add mood words last.** `quiet`, `uncanny`, `dreamlike`, `still` steer the tone after the scene is clear. The [emotion prompt guide](/blog/2026-02-23-master-ai-art-emotion-prompts) has more mood words.
- **Mix with another style.** Surreal ideas work in photography, oil painting or collage. See the [AI art styles list](/blog/2025-11-30-ai-art-styles-guide).
- **Remove clutter in the negative field.** `cluttered, busy background, extra objects` helps keep the scene clean. See the [negative prompt lists](/blog/2025-11-29-stable-diffusion-negative-prompts-guide).
